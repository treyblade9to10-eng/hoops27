HOOPS 27 sound credits
======================
Every sound effect in the game (ball bounce, swish, rim, backboard, buzzer, sneaker squeak, menu ticks, pack rip, card
reveals) is synthesized live in js/audio.js with the Web Audio API. No samples are used for those.

The three crowd files in this folder were copied from our own Bobble Bowl game. They were built from recordings that are
public domain (CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
CC0 means no permission or credit is needed; the sources are listed anyway so anyone can check them.
Freesound sounds were taken from their HQ preview files and cut, mixed, levelled and re-encoded to MP3.

crowd_bed.mp3 (arena crowd wash loop)
  "Crowd yelling huuuuu (60k persons) during a soccer game in Brazil" by felix.blume, CC0 - https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/612131/
  "noise#01.aif" (Feyenoord, De Kuip stadium, TV broadcast crowd mix) by huubjeroen, CC0 - https://freesound.org/people/huubjeroen/sounds/39733/
  "CRWDReac-SOCCER_Millerntor Stadium Crowd Reaction General Ambience 01" by itmightgetloud, CC0 - https://freesound.org/people/itmightgetloud/sounds/829454/
  "Milan Derby (Milan vs Inter) at San Siro Stadium" by neilraouf, CC0 - https://freesound.org/people/neilraouf/sounds/424354/

crowd_hype.mp3 (excited roar loop)
  "19 Football Crowd - Reaction To Goal.flac" by D.jones, CC0 - https://freesound.org/people/D.jones/sounds/528799/
  "CRWDReac-SOCCER_Millerntor Stadium Crowd Reaction General Ambience 01" by itmightgetloud, CC0 (see above)

crowd_fx.mp3 (one-shot reactions: cheers, ooh, claps, ref whistle)
  cheers:  "19 Football Crowd - Reaction To Goal.flac" by D.jones, CC0 (see above)
           "CRWDReac-SOCCER_Millerntor Stadium Crowd Reaction Excited 01" by itmightgetloud, CC0 - https://freesound.org/people/itmightgetloud/sounds/829453/
  ooh:     "Crowd yelling huuuuu (60k persons) during a soccer game in Brazil" by felix.blume, CC0 (see above)
  groan:   same felix.blume recording, plus "CRWDReac_Crowd Sigh In Disappointment_ShaneVincent_GSC24_FUMA-AmbeoVR.wav" by ShangusBurger, CC0 - https://freesound.org/people/ShangusBurger/sounds/764106/
  boos:    "JM_AMB_INT_Crowd Sport 01 - Booing.wav" by Julien_Matthey, CC0 - https://freesound.org/people/Julien_Matthey/sounds/557189/
           "CRWDAngr_Booing, Angry_ShaneVincent_GSC24_BIN-AmbeoVR.wav" by ShangusBurger, CC0 - https://freesound.org/people/ShangusBurger/sounds/763924/

  drum:    VSCO 2 CE concert bass drum by Versilian Studios, CC0 - https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE

File layout note: each file starts with a one-sample click followed by 0.25 s of silence (used to measure decoder delay).
